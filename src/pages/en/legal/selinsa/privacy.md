## 3. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information from users of the App.**

The App is a standalone application that operates entirely on the user's
device. We do not transmit or store any of the following information on any
servers or services we operate:

- The content, file names, or metadata of PDF files that users handle in the App
- User names, email addresses, or other contact information
- Device identifiers (including IDFA, AAID, or other advertising identifiers)
- Location information
- Contacts (address book) or photo library content
- Browsing history or search history
- Health or fitness information
- App usage statistics (operation logs, usage time, screen transitions, etc.)
- Crash reports or diagnostic information
- IP addresses or other communication metadata

**All PDF processing in the App (including imposition processing) is completed
locally on the user's device.**

### 3.1 Regarding Purchase Processing by App Stores

Purchase processing for the App is handled through each app store operated by
Apple Inc. and Google LLC. Information that users have registered with each
store (Apple ID, Google account, payment information, etc.) is handled by the
respective operator. This information is not disclosed to us.

We receive receipts or tokens indicating the fact of purchase from each store,
but these are used only for verification purposes within the App (on the user's
device) and are not transmitted to our servers.

For information about personal information handling at each store, please see:

- [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 3.2 Voluntary Provision via Contact Form

The App provides a contact form. If a user voluntarily contacts us through
this form, the content of the inquiry and the return contact information
(email address, etc.) are sent from the user's device directly to our email
address.

We use this information solely for responding to the inquiry, and delete it
within two years after the response is complete.

## 4. Purpose of Use

Information voluntarily provided by users under Section 3.2 is used only for
the following purposes:

- Responding to and addressing inquiries
- Improving the App (when aggregated in a non-identifying manner)

We do not use personal information for any other purposes.

## 5. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide collected personal information to third parties except in the
following cases:

- When we have obtained the user's consent
- When required by law
- When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property, and obtaining

## 6. Security Measures

We have implemented the following measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage
of information provided through the contact form:

- Deletion within a reasonable period after the inquiry has been addressed
